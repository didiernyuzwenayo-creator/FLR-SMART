Field Officer Guide

Step-by-step learning guide for Field Officers, from farmer engagement to survival assessment and reporting.

WORKING VERSION · REVIEW AS WE GO
This is a working learning guide built from the field procedures shared so far. It is not an officially approved Tubura / One Acre Fund SOP. Confirm internal rules, forms, deadlines and incentive calculations before operational use.

1. Project Engagement & Project Marketing

Purpose: Identify interested farmers and explain the FLR project clearly before enrollment.

  • Identify farmers interested in joining the project.
  • Explain the project structure, how it works and the benefits available to farmers.
  • Explain the role of trees, especially indigenous/native trees, in restoring hillsides, valleys and the wider landscape.
  • Explain that the project has an annual incentive linked to trees that have survived/established. Exact eligibility and payment rules must be confirmed from official project guidance.
2. Farmer Registration

Record initial farmer interest and the basic information required for the next enrollment step.

  • Farmer name.
  • Phone number and ID number.
  • Number of fields the farmer wants to plant.

Handle personal farmer information only in approved work tools and according to your organization's data-protection rules. Do not store real personal data in this public prototype.

3. Enrollment & Farm Measurement

Enrollment follows farmer interest. Field measurement is part of enrollment, not a separate later phase.

  • Measure the farmer's field area to determine the suitable planting package(s).
  • Capture enrollment information using COBO and ODK.
  • Confirm the exact package-selection rules and which tool captures each field with the official team; those details are not yet confirmed here.
4. FLR planting packages & spacing

The following measurements are project-specific standards shared by the Field Officer. Do not treat them as universal agroforestry spacing recommendations.

Contour

Plant trees along contour lines.

  • Large trees: 6 m apart.
  • Small shrubs: 50 cm spacing between large trees.
  • Purpose: contribute to indirect erosion control.

Boundary

Plant around the field boundary.

  • Large trees: 3 m apart.
  • Shrubs: 50 cm spacing between large trees.

Intercropping

Plant trees within the crop field.

  • Large trees arranged in a 6 m × 6 m grid.
  • One shrub halfway between two large trees, 3 m from each.
5. Farmer Group Formation & Leadership
  • Organize participating farmers into groups.
  • Select a leader for each group.
  • Use group leaders as a communication and coordination link with farmers.

The four groups previously discussed in the user's project context are Karongi Itoshye, Inshuti z'Igiti, Abarinzi z'Igiti and Intumwa z'Isi Itoshye. Confirm whether these are the groups for this specific FLR rollout before using them as a standard list.

6. Farmer Group Training
  • Train farmers on FLR and the purpose of the project.
  • Explain the available planting packages.
  • Explain the planting process and the responsibilities of participating farmers.
7. Pre-distribution technical training

Before seedlings are distributed, train farmers on:

  • Package layouts and spacing.
  • How each package should be planted.
  • Proper seedling planting methods.
8. Land Preparation

Confirmed sequence: farmers prepare their land before receiving seedlings.

Field Officers should explain and follow up on readiness. Specific land-preparation instructions (such as clearing, marking and hole dimensions) must follow the project's approved technical guidance; they have not yet been confirmed for this guide.

9. Distribution Card & Tree Distribution
  • Prepare/use the Distribution Card based on enrollment information.
  • Use the card as the reference for farmer, species and seedling quantities.
  • Organize distribution by farmer groups.
  • Check the farmer and allocation details against the approved distribution card.

Exact receipt confirmation and reconciliation steps should be added after the official process is confirmed.

10. Tree Planting Follow-up

Field Officers and group leaders jointly follow up after distribution.

  • Group leaders help check planting progress and communicate challenges.
  • Field Officers visit fields, provide technical support and follow up on planting.
11. Planting Survey

After distribution, conduct a Planting Survey. A key task confirmed so far is counting the seedlings planted. The purpose is to prepare for the later Survival Survey.

Planting Survey information is the baseline/reference used to plan the Survival Survey. Do not confuse planted seedlings with surviving trees.

12. Survival Survey (after one year)

The Survival Survey is conducted one year after planting, using the Planting Survey as its reference.

  • Count trees that survived/established.
  • Count trees that died.
  • Record the surviving-tree count for each farmer.

The exact sampling method, survey form, verification rules and calculation method still need confirmation from official project guidance.

13. Incentive Assessment

Survival Survey results are used to determine the number of surviving trees for each farmer and assess the incentive due.

Do not calculate or promise an incentive amount until the official rate, eligibility criteria, verification and payment rules have been provided.

14. Reporting

Field tasks and survey activities are reported through the required work reporting process. The specific report fields, deadlines and approval chain still need to be confirmed.

Items to confirm and improve

  • Official package selection rules.
  • Exact COBO and ODK workflows.
  • Official distribution reconciliation and receipt process.
  • Planting Survey form, coverage and required fields.
  • Survival Survey method and exact timing rules.
  • Incentive formula, eligibility and payment workflow.
  • Reporting formats, deadlines and approval responsibilities.

1. Project Engagement & Project Marketing

Intego: Kumenya abahinzi bashishikajwe n'umushinga no kubasobanurira uko ukora.

  • Kumenya abahinzi bifuza kwitabira umushinga.
  • Gusobanura uko umushinga uteye, uko ukora n'inyungu abahinzi bawukuramo.
  • Gusobanura akamaro k'ibiti, cyane cyane ibiti kavukire, mu gusana imisozi, ibibaya n'ubutaka muri rusange.
  • Gusobanura ko hari incentive ya buri mwaka ijyanye n'ibiti byafashe. Ibisabwa kugira ngo umuhinzi ayihabwe n'uko yishyurwa bigomba kwemezwa n'amabwiriza y'umushinga.
2. Farmer Registration — Kwandikisha abahinzi

Kwandika amakuru y'ibanze y'abahinzi bagaragaje ko bashishikajwe n'umushinga:

  • Amazina y'umuhinzi.
  • Numero ya telefone na numero y'irangamuntu.
  • Umubare w'imirima ashaka guteramo ibiti.

Amakuru bwite y'abahinzi agomba kubikwa mu bikoresho byemewe n'akazi. Ntukoreshe amakuru nyakuri muri iyi prototype rusange.

3. Enrollment & Farm Measurement — Kwiyandikisha no gupima umurima
  • Gupima ubuso bw'umurima kugira ngo hamenyekane package ikwiye.
  • Gukusanya amakuru ya enrollment ukoresheje COBO na ODK.
  • Uburyo nyabwo bwo guhitamo package n'icyo buri gikoresho gikora bizongerwamo bimaze kwemezwa.

Gupima umurima biri muri Enrollment; si icyiciro gikurikiraho ukwacyo.

4. FLR Packages — Packages n'intera yo gutera

Iyi ni intera yihariye y'umushinga wasobanuye; ntifatwe nk'amabwiriza rusange akoreshwa ahantu hose.

Contour

  • Ibiti binini: buri metero 6.
  • Utuyebwa (shrubs): buri 50 cm hagati y'ibiti binini.
  • Bigira uruhare mu kurwanya isuri mu buryo buziguye.

Boundary

  • Biterwa ku mbibi z'umurima.
  • Ibiti binini: buri metero 3.
  • Shrubs: buri 50 cm hagati y'ibiti binini.

Intercropping

  • Ibiti binini biteye mu murima mu murongo wa 6 m × 6 m.
  • Shrub imwe hagati y'ibiti binini bibiri, ku ntera ya 3 m kuri buri giti kinini.
5. Gushyiraho amatsinda no gutora abayobozi
  • Gushyira abahinzi mu matsinda.
  • Gutorera buri tsinda umuyobozi.
  • Abayobozi bafasha mu itumanaho no guhuza ibikorwa n'abahinzi.

Amatsinda ane yigeze kuvugwa ni Karongi Itoshye, Inshuti z'Igiti, Abarinzi z'Igiti na Intumwa z'Isi Itoshye. Tugomba kubanza kwemeza ko ari yo akoreshwa muri uyu mushinga.

6. Amahugurwa y'amatsinda y'abahinzi
  • Guhugura abahinzi kuri FLR n'intego z'umushinga.
  • Gusobanura packages zihari.
  • Gusobanura uburyo bwo gutera n'inshingano z'abahinzi.
7. Amahugurwa ya tekiniki mbere yo gutanga ingemwe
  • Gusobanura imiterere ya buri package n'intera yo gutera.
  • Kwerekana uko buri package iterwa.
  • Gusobanura uburyo bukwiye bwo gutera ingemwe.
8. Gutegura ubutaka

Urutonde rwemejwe: abahinzi babanza gutegura ubutaka mbere yo guhabwa ingemwe.

Field Officer akurikirana ko ubutaka bwiteguye. Uburyo burambuye bwo gutegura ubutaka n'ibipimo by'imyobo bizakurikiza amabwiriza ya tekiniki y'umushinga, nibimara gutangwa.

9. Distribution Card no gutanga ingemwe
  • Gukoresha Distribution Card itegurwa hashingiwe ku makuru ya Enrollment.
  • Iyo karita ni yo yifashishwa mu kumenya umuhinzi, ubwoko bw'ingemwe n'umubare agenerwa.
  • Gutanga ingemwe hakurikijwe amatsinda.
  • Kugenzura amakuru y'umuhinzi n'ayo agenewe kuri Distribution Card.
10. Gukurikirana igikorwa cyo gutera

Field Officer n'abayobozi b'amatsinda babikurikirana bafatanyije.

  • Abayobozi b'amatsinda bakurikirana aho gutera bigeze no kumenyesha ibibazo.
  • Field Officer asura imirima, agatanga ubufasha bwa tekiniki kandi agakomeza gukurikirana igikorwa.
11. Planting Survey

Nyuma yo kurangiza distribution hakorwa Planting Survey. Umurimo w'ingenzi wemejwe kugeza ubu ni ukubara ingemwe zatewe.

Amakuru ya Planting Survey ni yo yifashishwa mu gutegura Survival Survey. Ntitugomba kwitiranya ingemwe zatewe n'ibiti byafashe.

12. Survival Survey — nyuma y'umwaka umwe

Survival Survey ikorwa nyuma y'umwaka umwe uhereye igihe ibiti byatewe, yifashishije amakuru ya Planting Survey.

  • Kubara ibiti byafashe.
  • Kubara ibiti byapfuye.
  • Kwandika umubare w'ibiti byafashe kuri buri muhinzi.

Uburyo bwo guhitamo ibiti bisuzumwa, form, verification n'imibare ikoreshwa biracyakeneye kwemezwa n'amabwiriza y'umushinga.

13. Incentive Assessment — kugena incentive

Ibyavuye muri Survival Survey bifasha kumenya umubare w'ibiti byafashe kuri buri muhinzi no kugena incentive agomba guhabwa.

Ntitugomba kubara cyangwa gusezeranya amafaranga runaka hataraboneka igipimo, ibisabwa n'uburyo bwo kwishyura byemewe.

14. Reporting — Raporo

Ibikorwa byo mu murima na survey bitangirwa raporo hakurikijwe uburyo busabwa n'akazi. Imiterere ya raporo, igihe cyo kuyitanga n'abayemeza bizongerwamo bimaze kwemezwa.

Amakuru azakosorwa cyangwa akongerwamo

  • Uburyo bwo guhitamo package.
  • Uko COBO na ODK bikoreshwa.
  • Uko distribution yemezwa n'uko ingemwe zakiriwe byandikwa.
  • Form n'amakuru akenewe muri Planting Survey.
  • Uburyo bwa Survival Survey n'igihe nyacyo ikorerwa.
  • Formula ya incentive n'ibisabwa kugira ngo itangwe.
  • Uburyo bwo gutanga raporo, igihe ntarengwa n'abazemeza.
